Field guide

Keeping a settlement

Hands & homes

Settlers sleep in houses and work where you place them. Anyone without a post becomes a labourer, who carries materials and raises new buildings. Keep a few spare or nothing gets built.

Bread & firewood

Each settler eats once a day: fish, berries or bread. More kinds of food make happier people. Winter halts the farms and every house burns wood to keep warm.

Chains of trade

Logs become planks at the sawmill. Planks and stone become tools at the workshop. Wheat goes to the windmill, flour to the bakery. Workers without tools work slowly.

Growth

Spare beds, full larders and a happy village bring new settlers, and settler ships come each spring. New buildings unlock as the village grows.

The merchant

Build a dock and a merchant calls at the start of every season. Sell your surplus for gold and buy what the island lacks.

Raiders

Longships come from the open sea. Watchtowers shoot from range, and a barracks trains a militia. When the horn sounds, settlers run for the town hall and work stops.
