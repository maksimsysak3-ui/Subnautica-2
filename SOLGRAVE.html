DIRECTIVE
CAMPAIGN Six chapters, six worlds, six Wardens. Clear each to break the next seal in the Chronicle and unlock its sector for Endless patrol.
ENDLESS Hold any unlocked sector as long as you can. Waves escalate forever; a Warden surfaces every fifth wave. Night runs hotter — watch your scanner.
HANGAR Spend cores on new glaives and refits. Every hull flies differently.
OVERDRIVE
Every kill charges your glaive's OVERDRIVE. When the bar fills, hit F (or right-click) to unleash your ship's signature ultimate — each hull's is different. Save it for a Warden.
FIELD SUPPLIES
Green medkits appear across the sector and drop from kills — fly over them to patch your hull. The scanner marks them green.
SALVAGE
⬡ Cores drop from elites, wardens and survived waves. Spend them in the Armory between runs. Every sector is generated fresh from its seed.
